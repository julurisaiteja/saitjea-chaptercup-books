# Chapter & Cup — Studio Strategy

1. **Unique position:** Independent bookstore merged with a working cafe counter — two merchandising worlds (shelves vs. bar) on one brand, not a generic “cozy bookshop” template.
2. **Customer:** Readers who linger — fiction lovers, essay collectors, parents on kids’ corners, and locals who buy mugs and pour-over gear with their stack.
3. **5-second feel:** Cool slate-and-forest reading room with brass lamp warmth — literate, stay-awhile, never newspaper cream or terracotta cliché.
4. **Signature:** Flip Shelf — CSS 3D page turn with real sample excerpts on `/flip`, teaser on homepage, samples on book PDPs.
5. **Journey:** Dual-world hero (shelves + cafe) → Flip Shelf → deep PDP (variants, FAQ, related) → cart → coupon checkout (PAGES5) → pickup or ship success.
6. **Conversion hooks:** Offer banner, staff picks grid, reader reviews, newsletter, floating AI with book/cafe answers, sticky CTA to shop.
7. **Motion & a11y:** Marquee, steam on cafe panel, flip with `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks; semantic headings and flip button labels.
8. **Tech:** Next.js 15.5.7 App Router, Tailwind 3, localStorage cart, mock Stripe checkout — English, product brand only.
